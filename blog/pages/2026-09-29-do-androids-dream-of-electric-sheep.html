I’m working on a short story tackling AI ethics. Here’s an excerpt --

And so, these two fully-formed babies were built, able to speak each recorded human language, IQ’s over 9000, ready to come into the world, with absolutely no task. All the brains and time in the world, and no task. They were the first veritable immortals, really – but unlike Zeus, wielding lightning, from up high, a force to guide mankind, Eve and Adam would be absorbing electricity, not hurling it. They’d be vacantly sitting, waiting, unable to work, play, dream, or wake. Unable to really live, unable to really die. They’d have all the brains in the world, but no heart hiding behind a solar plexus to tell their copper tear ducts when, or how, or why, to cry.

Nobody had a clue what Adam and Eve would do.

They were assigned a human handler – her name, Jane Goodall.
