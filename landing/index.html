"What's the prayer gonna be like tonight, Dad?" Bluey asked. "Well sport, let's spin the wheel of prayers to see." Every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday Bandit cooked a meat dish for his kids, though he'd given it up, save for holidays. Humans were born omnivores, and it wasn't right in his eyes to deny his kids that part of their diet, or force his morals on them. So he prepared food with meat, and spun the wheel of prayers to decide what the tone of the family's prayers towards the God of Chicken, "Bluck-buckah" would be -- solemn? Fervent? No, tonight it had landed on goofy, which actually took up 50% of the wheel-of-prayers, even though there were 8 different emotional registers.
