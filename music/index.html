Here are my current albums. That's all I've got to display at the moment.

I'm thinking out how to best organize what other music I have.

◈ Lovely Spectrum

August 14, 2025

Music touching on themes relating to neurodivergence, or the autism "spectrum", and the more pure love those folks tend to exibit. Each song also corresponds to a color; brown, then the colors of the rainbow, in order.

◢ Infrared

March 6, 2025

An album addressing the ultimate disappointment of settling in relationships for carnal satisfaction. The imagery is evocative of mankind looking up at a red sky, wishing to see the blood moon, bleeding down. Forewarning: the first song is probably the worst thing I've made. The rest came out well.

◉ Fate & Fiction

January 30, 2025

An album addressing the tension between imagined feelings and real experiences in the wake of a strong friendship, bordering on a relationship, ending. Mostly relatable to those in the aro-ace world, perhaps.

☼ Gerudo Daydream

September 21, 2024

Not much more than a mixtape based on a Zelda Game -- Twilight Princess. The songs are all over the place. One theme tying them together is liminality, as at twilight. Perhaps fragmentation of emotion, as well.
