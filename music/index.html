Anywho, again, here are my current albums. I'm thinking out how to best organize the other pieces of music mentioned.

◈ Lovely Spectrum

August 14, 2025

Music touching on themes relating to neurodivergence, or the autism "spectrum", and love. Each song also corresponds to a color; brown, then the colors of the rainbow, in order.

◢ Infrared

March 6, 2025

Songs with a red-tinged view on the unsatisfying nature of casual relationships in our culture. The title is, perhaps, evocative of a blood moon, bleeding down.

◉ Fate & Fiction

January 30, 2025

An album addressing the tension between imagined feelings and real experiences in the wake of a strong friendship, bordering on a relationship, ending. Mostly relatable to those in the aro-ace world, perhaps.

☼ Gerudo Daydream

September 21, 2024

Not much more than a mixtape based on a Zelda Game -- Twilight Princess. The songs are all over the place. One theme tying them together is liminality, as at twilight. Perhaps fragmentation of emotion, as well.
