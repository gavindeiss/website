Untitled

I just wonder why they don’t teach us in school
  How unimportant talking really is.
   It’s almost all nonverbal, how we speak after
   25.

The clanging of keys, jangling from my pants, on a  
 Carabiner.

The click of a coworker’s computer-keyboard taps.

The calm cadence of your knife hitting a cutting board after your partner
  Fluffed the couch cushion with just the right rigor.

The disquieting clatter of a silver spoon, 
   thrown just slightly too hard,
  Into the dishwasher,
 After hearing your love scuff their tennis shoes on the linoleum –
  They know you don’t like that.

 It's all undecipherable morse code that
 Breaks the binary.
