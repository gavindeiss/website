To any of my millions of fans that don't live in Vermont and are thus out of the loop -- there's a big vote around the capital city regarding whether or not to:
    1. build a Tech Center next to our high school with a strong arts-leaning; 
    2. to instead repurpose existing facilities, i.e. schools that are on the chopping block to be closed; or 
    3. to just not build a tech center at all.

I’ve been brushing up on George Orwell’s masterpiece, ‘2026’; er sorry, typo — ‘1984’, and there were a few things I forgot from that book that really would’ve helped me keep my last few jobs. All that the ‘doublespeak’ stuff, namely – that stuff about how to use lingo in the workplace and whatnot.

I don’t ever wanna see another person in the same situation as me: out of work, with no Agency` to get a new job, cuz they don’t talk the talk. I’m jobless, without purpose, with no Reservations about the fact that I’m a worthless bum. I just wish I’d bought a cheap Jeep Cherokee before I lost my last job, rather than a Lone Ranger, to reduce the financial strain on my parents.

That’s why I’m proposing: the “double-doublespeak” amendment. 

A quick refresher folks: a google search will tell ya that ‘doublespeak’ is all about using vague, overly-complicated language to obscure the truth. For example, hearing a news anchor saying the phrase “meaningful downturn in aggregate output" to avoid saying the word "recession"? Now that’s doublespeak. 

Or take another example – when you hear an ‘investigative journalist’ hide behind the vague language of statistics to say “education costs have risen 40% since covid” instead of investigating an answer to the question “uhm… risen from… what, exactly? From hiring more people at the Agency` of Education, to research exactly how many iPads and Chromebooks to buy for each student that’s leaving the state?” – now that’s Champlain-approved doublespeak.

Surely, there’s a way around this, yeah? Aren’t we all just frickin tired of all this doublespeak? Ain’t there another option? There sure is folks, there sure is. It’s my biggest, most beautifulest idea ever: double-double speak.

Double-doublespeak is nothing like Doublespeak. It gets past all the nonsense, the codespeak and gobbledygook, in a technique I like to call: “lying”. For example, saying “The nasdaq rose 6.7% this quarter” instead of saying “every other person you know is either working 3 jobs or doesn’t have one at all” – now that’s double-doublespeak. Ain’t that better?
Or, for an example that’s closer to home, with the Tech Center – when you hear folks argue “It would actually cost more to repurpose existing facilities like EMES” instead of saying “Repurposing EMES will require us to use our public money to hire private contractors that claim it costs 1 million dollars to install each new ‘totally necessary, Tech-Center Conformant’ window”, now crikey – that’s a real beauty example of double-doublespeak. I’d cast an Australian ballot on that, I would.

Now listen up, folks, I’m not gonna “double-doublespeak” at y’all right now, I’m gonna tell it true for a second: I think the new tech center is a real triple-double idea, and not one of them dumb, commie-eurostep, James Harden triple-doubles neither, nah, it’s a real beastmode, Giannis, Greek-Freak Milwaukee Buck one. But —
Are we really gonna build a new top-of-the-line trades school next to a top-of-the-line arts school, just to keep segregating the folks learning trades from the folks learning books? That’s awful sad. At my first blue-collared job I ever worked, one of the fellas told me it broke his heart that his daughter had to drop chorus as she went off from public school to trades school to learn to cut hair. She loved to sing. And there ain’t really nowhere to sing these days. Church ain’t politically correct, to tell it true.
And even worse, cuz she went off to cut hair, she won’t have the opportunity to read 1984 in an English class! 
Crikey! 
Now, I don’t mean to cause a ruckus and keep this tech center from being built at all, constantly delayed, but tell me — do we have a plan to share facilities? After the vote, if it gets approved, can we add a double-doublespeak amendment ensuring that the trades folks can still take english classes, so they can not-not read 1984?
